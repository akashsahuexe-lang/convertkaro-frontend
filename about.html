The Convertkaro Story

Convertkaro was born out of everyday digital frustration. Whether you are a student trying to compress a photo and signature for a government exam, or a professional needing to merge PDF files securely, the tools available online were either cluttered with ads or demanded expensive subscriptions.

Founded by Akash Sahu, a design professional based in Kanpur, India, Convertkaro.in was built to solve these exact roadblocks. Our platform provides high-quality, fully accurate utility tools—including an advanced Hindi Font Converter (Unicode to Kruti Dev / 4CGandhi) crafted specifically for local publishers and typists.

Our philosophy is straightforward: provide 100% free PDF utilities and image tools that process your files directly inside your browser. This means your data is never uploaded to external servers, ensuring maximum privacy and lightning-fast speeds.
